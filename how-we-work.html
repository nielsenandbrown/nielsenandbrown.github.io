How we think about it

Five questions behind every AI decision.

So every decision is about business value, not guesswork.

  1. Where could it help?

    Which workflows and daily jobs could AI make faster or lighter?

  2. Where is the real value?

    Which of those save leadership time, cut risk or improve client delivery?

  3. Does it pay off?

    What will it save or earn back, and is that worth the spend?

  4. Are you ready?

    Can your systems, data and team support it, or do gaps need fixing first?

  5. Is it safe and scalable?

    How do we keep it secure, controlled and improving as you grow?

We call this the AI Business Strategy Wheel. You just get the five questions, worked through together.
