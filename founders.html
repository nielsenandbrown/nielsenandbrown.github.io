Who you'll work with

Allan Nielsen, Co-founder

Allan has spent his career working with the businesses that keep the UK moving. He's led commercial teams, supported B2B product and service providers, and helped teams solve practical problems.

Throughout, he's been drawn to how small gains in technology, and now AI, change the way people work. That goes back to his years as a competitive swimmer.

“The gap between good and exceptional is rarely one big leap. It is a hundred small gains, compounded.”

That's how good AI adoption works too. Not one big, chaotic overhaul, but steady improvements that add up.

Allan moved from Denmark to build his life and career in the UK. He's a husband and a father to three boys, and does his best thinking on an early morning workout.
