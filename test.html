How we think about it

Five straightforward questions to make sure every AI decision drives clear business value rather than guesswork.

We call this the AI Business Strategy Wheel internally. You just get the five questions, worked through together.

1. Where could it help?

Which team workflows and daily operations can AI make faster, lighter, or more efficient?

2. Where is the real value?

Which of those areas will actually save leadership time, cut operating risks, or improve client delivery?

3. Does it pay off?

What will it realistically save or earn back, and does the return justify the investment?

4. Are you ready?

Do your current systems, data, and team workflows support AI adoption, or do foundational gaps need fixing first?

5. Is it safe and scalable?

How do we keep AI solutions secure, controlled, and continuously improving as your company grows?
