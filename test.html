How we think about it

Five plain questions we ask about every business

We call this the AI Business Strategy Wheel internally. To you, it is five straightforward questions, worked through together, so every decision has a clear reason behind it rather than a guess.

1. Where could it help

Which parts of the business could AI realistically make faster or easier.

2. Where is the real value

Which of those would actually save time, improve service, or cut risk.

3. Does it pay off

What would it save or earn back, and is that worth the investment.

4. Are we ready

Do the systems, data, and people support it, or does something need fixing first.

5. Staying safe

How it keeps improving, and stays under control as it grows.
