Quick answers

Do we have to sign up to a big project?

No. Everything is fixed scope and fixed price, and most people start with the free score or a £995 half day. You only move on if the first step was worth it.

We're not sure we're ready for AI. Is that a problem?

That's where most of our clients start. The readiness score tells you honestly, and sometimes the answer is "not yet". We'll say so.

How do you keep our data safe?

We agree what data can be used, and where, before anything is built. A person checks anything customer-facing, and it's all written down in a plain-English policy.

Do you push a particular AI tool?

No. We're not paid by any software company. We build with whatever fits, often tools you already pay for.
