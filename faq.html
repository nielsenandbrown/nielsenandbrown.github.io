About us

What does Nielsen & Brown actually do?

We help UK businesses with 10 to 300 people use AI in practical ways. We find where it helps, get your team using it, build tools where nothing off the shelf fits, and put basic safety in place.

Is this only for big companies?

No, the opposite. We work with businesses of roughly 10 to 300 people. Most AI consultancies are built for much bigger firms, and freelancers rarely cover strategy, training and building at once. We sit in between, priced for a business your size.

Will this feel like a big consultancy engagement?

No. No long sales process, no room full of junior analysts, no report that sits unread. You deal with us directly: a short diagnostic, a working tool, and a trained team. Most first projects are live within six to eight weeks.

How are you different from a freelance AI consultant?

A freelancer is usually one person with one specialism. We cover strategy, training, building and governance, so you don't have to coordinate several suppliers or manage the hand-offs between them.

Where are you based, and do you work across the UK?

We're based at Bartle House, Oxford Court, Manchester, and work with businesses across the UK. Most of the work is remote. For anything that needs to be on site, like in-person sessions, we travel.

Cost and getting started

How much does it cost?

Every piece of work has a fixed scope and a fixed price, published up front. The smallest first step is the AI Starting Point at £995, half a day on site. Bigger pieces, like the Diagnostic Assessment at £4,500, come once a first step has shown the idea is worth doing. See every price. The first call is free, with no obligation.

What does a typical engagement look like?

Most businesses start with the free AI Readiness Score. The next step is small, fixed and specific. If it doesn't earn the next one, you stop there. Bigger work, like automation at scale or a full diagnostic and roadmap, follows once a first step has proved its worth. See how we work.

What's the best first project for a business our size?

Usually something small, repetitive and done often: handling documents, answering routine customer questions, or preparing reports. A short session finds the one with the clearest payback, and we prove it works before you commit to anything bigger.

Should we buy AI software or build a custom tool?

Buy where a good off-the-shelf tool fits how your team works. Build where it doesn't. We're independent of any software company, so we recommend what fits your business, not what we're paid to sell.

Data and safety

How do we keep our data safe?

With a bit of planning, not guesswork. We agree what data can be used and where, pick tools that fit your level of risk, keep a person checking anything sensitive, and write it all down so you can show how AI is used.

What AI governance does a business our size actually need?

At minimum: an AI usage policy, a simple risk register, clear owners for decisions, a person checking anything customer-facing or higher risk, and a record of how AI is used. That baseline comes with every engagement, not as an add-on. How we keep AI safe.
